## Overview

This repository provides the supplementary datasets and figures accompanying the doctoral thesis *Genomic Evolution of Livestock Associated Staphylococcus aureus: Host Adaptation and Rabbit Lineage Emergence*. The research integrates comparative genomics, phylogenomics, pangenome analysis, antimicrobial resistance and virulence profiling, mobile genetic element characterization, molecular dating, and functional assays.

The thesis comprises three interconnected research chapters:

1. **Chapter 1 — Comparative genomics across six livestock hosts:** 1,884 *S. aureus* genomes from pigs, cattle, rabbits, poultry, goats and sheep; population structure, clonal redundancy, pangenome, resistome, virulome and mobilome.
2. **Chapter 2 — Rabbit-associated *S. aureus*:** 358 isolates; population structure, phylogenomics, accessory genome dynamics and experimental characterization of virulence-associated deletion mutants.
3. **Chapter 3 — ST121/ST3764 evolution and prophages:** 196 rabbit-associated CC121 genomes (134 ST121 and 62 ST3764); evolutionary history, prophages PR and 307, and phage-susceptibility assays.

## Data formats and interpretation

- **Tables:** Microsoft Excel (`.xlsx`); **figures:** PNG (`.png`).
- For the Chapter 1 virulence matrix (S4), **1** indicates detected/present and **0** indicates not detected/absent under the study's annotation approach. Detection does not demonstrate expression or phenotype.
- The plasmid mobility categories, prophage/SaPI/ICE markers and predicted phage packaging strategy reflect **sequence-based inference** unless explicitly validated experimentally in the thesis.
- Some source metadata are incomplete. Missing or unassigned values should be interpreted according to the relevant spreadsheet and thesis methods.
- The supplementary files should be interpreted alongside the methods, results and limitations reported in the corresponding thesis chapters.
